When internet fails, send a formatted SMS to the RALCA Command Number. Our gateway parses it and updates the Firebase DB.
SMS FORMAT:
RALCA | SOS | <TYPE> | <LAT> | <LNG> | <DESC>
Example:
RALCA | SOS | FLOOD | 13.08 | 80.27 | Road submerged need boats
